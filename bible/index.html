The Berean Standard Bible

The whole Berean Standard Bible (BSB), one page to a chapter: 66 books, 1,189 chapters and 31,086 verses, with the translators’ section headings and footnotes. (31,086 rather than the traditional 31,102, because the BSB, following the critical Greek text, does not print sixteen verses in its running text; each chapter’s notes say where.)
