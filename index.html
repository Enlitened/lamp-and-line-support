The Hebrew and Greek behind every word

Tap any verse to see the Hebrew or Greek behind every word: how it is said, how the translators rendered it, and its grammar in plain words. Tap a word for its dictionary entry, every way the Berean Standard Bible renders it, and every place it occurs.

Part of Lamp & Line Full. The words come from the translators’ own tables, the entries from STEP Bible’s lexicons.

The Bible on Apple Watch

Yours, and private

Nothing you read, search for, or write is sent to us, and there is no analytics of any kind. Bookmarks, highlights, notes, and plans are kept on your device and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud database with every field encrypted, so your iPhone and iPad match; your place in the text is offered, not imposed. Searches and settings never go to iCloud. You can export everything you have written, read it back in on another device, and delete it, at any time.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account, and no way to make one.

Does it work without the internet?

Yes. The whole Bible is stored on your device, and so is the daily reading, so reading never needs a connection. iCloud Sync, if you turn it on, uses one to keep your devices in step, and buying Full goes through the App Store.

What translation is it?

The Berean Standard Bible, which was placed into the public domain on 30 April 2023. The exact source edition is shown in Settings.

Is the Bible free?

On iPhone and iPad, yes, always: the complete text, search, your bookmarks, your notes, Today, read aloud, one reading plan (the New Testament, or the whole Bible in a year), and every accessibility feature. On Apple Watch, Today is free: the day’s Psalm, its reading, and its prayer, with your phone switched off. Reading the whole Bible on the Watch is part of Full.

Is Lamp & Line Full a subscription?

No. Full is a single purchase, never a subscription. Lamp & Line is one app for iPhone and iPad, and one purchase covers both.

Is the Bible text in my language?

The app itself is in ten languages: English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Dutch, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese. The Bible text is the English Berean Standard Bible. It is public domain, which is the reason the whole Bible can be in the app, offline and free; other translations are licensed. So you navigate and search in your language, and read Scripture in English.
