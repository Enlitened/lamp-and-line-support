What it leaves out

  • No advertising. None in the reader, none on Today, none anywhere.
  • No account. There is no account, and no way to make one.
  • No analytics. Nothing you read, search for, highlight, or write is sent to us. The App Store label reads “Data Not Collected”.
  • No feed. Today is one card a day: a Psalm, a reading and a prayer.
  • No subscription. The Bible is free. Lamp & Line Full is one purchase.

Where what you write is kept

Your bookmarks, highlights, notes, the days you have marked complete, your recent searches and your settings live in a database inside the app’s own storage on your device. You can export all of it from Settings, read it back in on another device, and delete all of it, at any time.

iCloud Sync is off until you turn it on. When it is on, your marks and plans are kept in step between your iPhone and iPad through your own private iCloud database, with every field encrypted. We cannot read any of it: a private iCloud database is yours, and there is nowhere we could open it. Your searches and settings never go to iCloud.

What is sent anywhere

Nothing, with three exceptions that are not us. If you buy Lamp & Line Full or leave a tip, Apple processes that. If you use the Watch app, your preferences and bookmark list pass between your iPhone and your Watch over Apple’s own connection between your paired devices. And iCloud Sync, if you switch it on, goes through your own iCloud. The privacy policy sets all of this out in full.

How it can be free

The text is the Berean Standard Bible, which was placed into the public domain on 30 April 2023, so there is no licence to pay for and nothing to recover with advertising. The whole of it is stored on your device, so there is no server to run either: it reads offline.

On iPhone and iPad the complete Bible, search, read aloud, bookmarks, whole-verse highlights, notes, Today and a reading plan cost nothing, for good. Lamp & Line Full is one purchase, never a subscription. It adds word study, iCloud sync, threads between related passages, every page and typeface, every share-card design, as many reading plans as you like, and the whole Bible on Apple Watch.

This website

The site has no analytics, no cookies and no advertising. Its web host, Vercel, sees a request for a page as any web host does.

Questions people ask

Do I need an account?
No. There is no account, and no way to make one.
Does the app show advertisements?
No. There is no advertising anywhere in it.
What does the app collect?
Nothing. There is no analytics of any kind, and the App Store label reads “Data Not Collected”.
Can anyone see what I read or search for?
No. Search runs entirely against the copy of the Bible stored on your device, and your searches are never transmitted. Nothing you read, highlight, or write is sent to us.
Is it a subscription?
No. The Bible is free on iPhone and iPad, for good. Lamp & Line Full is a single purchase, never a subscription.
