What Today is

One Psalm, one short reading, one prayer. One card a day, not a feed. The Psalm and the reading are from the Berean Standard Bible; the prayers are original writing that a person reviewed.

It opens under a landscape painted in the light of the hour: dawn, day, dusk or night. The hours are fixed ones rather than your sunrise and sunset, because working those out would need your location, and the app does not ask for it.

Where it shows up

  • In the app, on iPhone and iPad, as the first tab.
  • On your Home Screen and Lock Screen, as a widget. A Home Screen widget is free, in StandBy too; every other size is part of Lamp & Line Full.
  • On Apple Watch, with your phone switched off. The Watch carries its own copy of the daily readings and works out what day it is by itself. More about the Watch app.
  • In Control Centre and on the Action button. Today’s Lamp is one of the app’s two Controls, and it is free.

One reminder, or none

Lamp & Line offers one reminder a day, at a time you choose, or none at all. It is a local notification scheduled on your device; there is no push service of ours.

It works offline, and it is private

The daily readings are stored on your device with the whole Bible, so Today never needs a connection. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics.

After Today

If you want more than a Psalm a day, a reading plan sits on Today beneath the day’s prayer: the New Testament, or the whole Bible in a year, a few chapters at a time and with no dates to fall behind.

Questions people ask

Is the daily reading free?
Yes. Today is free on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch: the day’s Psalm, its reading, and its prayer.
Does it need a connection?
No. The daily readings are stored on your device, so Today works offline.
What language is it in?
The app is in ten languages and the theme of the day is translated. The Psalm, the reading and the prayer are in English, like the rest of Scripture in the app.
Can I get a reminder?
Yes: one a day, at a time you choose, or none at all.
